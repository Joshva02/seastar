Welcome to Sea Star

Come and relax with us

Sea Star is the latest addition to the ultra-luxurious villas on the world-famous Blue Lagoon strip. Newly built and impeccably appointed, the villa sits at the water's edge — every bedroom framed by panoramic views of the Caribbean Sea.

A dedicated staff of three — your private chef, butler and housekeeper — pamper you with first-class service throughout your stay, from sunrise breakfasts on the sea deck to candlelit dinners under the stars.

Sea Star is the perfect setting for a family vacation or a secluded, quiet romantic getaway. Spend your days swimming, kayaking or just lounging and soaking up those vibrant Jamaican rays.

Beyond the villa

Experiences in Port Antonio

Serene and calm at the villa — endless adventure just beyond it. Your butler will gladly arrange any of these for you.

The Blue Lagoon

A lush, tropical freshwater paradise reaching depths of over 180 feet, where layers of cool spring water meet the warm sea — right on your doorstep.

Monkey Island

Beautiful Monkey Island is just a short kayak ride from the villa. Enjoy the secluded island and make mud masks on its shore.

Frenchman's Cove

A river winding through lush vegetation to an idyllic beach — famous for the fireflies and luminescent insects that light it up at night.

Reach Falls

An exotic waterfall on the John Crow Mountains, cascading into an emerald pool with an underwater cave and guided river tours upstream.

San San & Boston Beach

White sand and shallow, child-friendly water at San San; twenty minutes east, Boston Beach — the birthplace of jerk pork.

Somerset Falls

Near Hope Bay, the Daniels River cascades through a rocky gorge into a pool surrounded by tropical rainforest.
